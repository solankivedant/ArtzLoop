How ArtzLoop works

A 2-minute guide - everything here works the same in all 8 toys.

This toy

Draw a stroke and it mirrors instantly into a snowflake, and finishing one sets off a soft icy shimmer. Symmetry sets how many copies (1 turns it off entirely; 6 is the classic snowflake look); Mirror folds each copy again. Switch to the eraser, pick its own size, and pick a mode - trim just what you touch, remove a whole symmetric piece or the whole stroke at once, or erase a filled area (just the part you touch, or every mirrored copy of it). Switch to fill and tap any area closed off by strokes to flood it with the current color, mirrored the same way as a stroke. Switch to recolor and hover over any stroke or filled area - it highlights to show exactly what you're about to hit - then click to repaint it with the current color; pick "This copy only" to retint just the one symmetric copy under your cursor, or "Whole piece" to retint every copy of it at once.

The canvas

Draw with mouse, touch or a pen - they all work the same way. The canvas is effectively infinite: it grows as you draw past its edge, so you don't need to worry about running out of room. The faint grid lines are just a framing guide - they're never saved or exported.

Top bar

↺ / ↻ undo and redo (Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y). Dark / Light flips the canvas background. Grid toggles the guide grid. Download opens a menu where you can rename the file, then save a .art project file to resume later, or export a flat PNG, JPEG, or a transparent PNG with no background. Upload reopens a saved .art file (or just drag one onto the canvas). Clear wipes the canvas. The dropdown and speaker icon pick and mute the background music.

Bottom zoom bar

Drag its grip (⋮⋮) to move it anywhere - it turns vertical if you drop it near the left or right edge. The hand icon toggles pan mode (or just hold Space) to drag the view around; the dashed-corner icon toggles select mode; − and + zoom by a fixed step, and the percentage between them is a text field - click it and type any exact zoom level. ⤢ fits your whole drawing on screen.

Select mode

Toggling it on selects your whole drawing at once. Two buttons appear: a trash icon to delete it, and a rotate handle below it - drag the handle to spin the art around the snowflake's center. The canvas grid never moves, only the art does.

Side tool panel

Drag its grip to move it too - horizontal at the bottom, vertical at the sides. It holds this toy's own controls: numeric values use − / + steppers you can also type into directly, toggles are simple switches, and the color swatch opens a palette of presets plus a Custom color picker (drag the gradient square and hue strip, or type a hex code).

Saving your work

Download → Project file gives you a real save you can reopen anytime via Upload. This browser also autosaves roughly every 10 seconds in the background - if you come back to a page with unsaved work, it'll offer to resume it.

Keyboard shortcuts

Ctrl+S quick-save, Ctrl+O upload, Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y undo/redo, Ctrl+M toggle Mirror, Alt+C open the color picker, B / E / F / R / S switch to Brush, Eraser, Fill, Recolor or Select, Delete removes the current selection, Esc exits select mode.
